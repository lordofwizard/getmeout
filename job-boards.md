# Job boards — AI Engineer roles in DE / NL / UK / FR / IT (+ remote)

Researched 2026-09-28. Paste any posting URL or text into chat; the agent tailors, compiles, and logs it.
Visa figures are gross; recheck the official page before accepting an offer.

## Where you have the best odds (your profile: B.E. 2025, AI agents/MCP, Rust/C++/Java, robotics, needs sponsorship)

| Rank | Country | Why | Visa route + salary floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Germany** | Largest English-friendly tech market; IT is a shortage occupation | EU Blue Card: ~€45,934/yr for shortage occupations incl. IT (general ~€50,700) — [make-it-in-germany.com](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/eu-blue-card). No offer yet? Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte) lets you job-hunt on site |
| 2 | **Netherlands** | English is the default in tech; fast permit via IND-recognised sponsors; Vanderlande (NL HQ) on your CV is recognised here | Highly skilled migrant: €4,357/mo under 30, €5,942/mo 30+ (excl. 8% holiday pay, Jul–Dec 2026) — [ind.nl amounts](https://ind.nl/en/required-amounts-income-requirements). **Employer must be on the [IND recognised sponsor register](https://ind.nl/en/public-register-recognised-sponsors/public-register-work)** |
| 3 | **United Kingdom** | Strong AI scene (London); every sponsor is publicly listed | Skilled Worker: £41,700 or the occupation "going rate", whichever is higher — [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/your-job). Check employer on the [register of licensed sponsors](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers). New-entrant discount may apply — verify |
| 4 | **France** | Paris AI hub (Station F, many LLM startups); many roles still expect French | Talent Passport or EU Blue Card; the "salarié qualifié" category usually expects a master's-level degree, so confirm which category fits a bachelor's before applying |
| 5 | **Italy** | Smallest English-language AI market, lower pay, Italian often required; opportunistic only | EU Blue Card (threshold revised yearly — verify) |

## Daily drivers (cover all 5 countries — start here)

- **LinkedIn Jobs** — linkedin.com/jobs — highest volume everywhere. Search `"AI Engineer" OR "LLM Engineer" OR "Applied AI"`, location = country, add `visa sponsorship` or `relocation` to keywords, set daily alerts.
- **Welcome to the Jungle** (Otta merged in) — welcometothejungle.com — best UX for FR/UK/EU startups, shows language + remote policy.
- **Relocate.me** — relocate.me — only roles with relocation/visa support; filter by country.
- **Arbeitnow** — arbeitnow.com (also arbeitnow.co.uk, arbeitnow.fr) — dedicated "Visa sponsorship", "English speaking", "Relocation package" filters.
- **Foorilla** (formerly ai-jobs.net; old URL redirects) — foorilla.com — AI/ML/data only, low noise.
- **Wellfound** — wellfound.com — startups; filter "Visa sponsorship" and "Remote".

## United Kingdom
