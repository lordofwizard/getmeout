# I am done

I don't want to be happy again,

Some trouble would lead me to pain. 

Those nights I don't want to go through, 

when there is no one to understand you. 

Crying out and yelling at everyone so bad, 

I am freaking out and completely going mad. 

Want to live a life so free, 

where I can smile and glee. 

Trouble knocking my life's door every time I sense joy, 

hey you, unhappiness am I your favorite toy? 

Guilt enhances your fear of pain, 

please, that's it, get me out of this unending hurricane

>  Ayushi Pundalik
