# Response

1. what happened during veritas thingy

    1. They were basically already asking me docs and everything for my onboarding, and i didn't want to commit to a particular company at that point.

    2. So told this to pallavi, and she told she will remove me from the placements

    3. Still i did sneak into few placements, but after landing fampay i kinda stopped going to interviews and stuff

2. Reaching atlan

    1. I knew about atlan since first year, a friend of friend was working there

    2. We became friends in 3rd year when he came to hangout with us, after that we kept in touch

    3. He gave me the referral

3. Landing fulltime job

    1. What you did in your internship matters more

    2. In both internships i worked on SDE level, they never felt like internships

    3. So during atlan interview, they could gauge that i had industry experience and i was ready to take on full time responsibilities

4. Keep vanderlande as a backup

    1. Don't reject them upfront, keep your intensions hidden, its fine if you ditch them later on

    2. College will bother you if you try to leave this offer, so just play it cool (although if you do reject it, you will get street cred++)

5. Transitioning from intern mindset to fulltime is like a marathon than a sprint, find your pace, your missions and thats it :)

    1. I struggled with not having anyone to monitor me closely, i was supposed to pick my own tasks and stuff, so you need probably work on that once you become full time (you will have your reporting folks, although become autonomous, thats the road to sde 2)

6. Find a number that you feel like you deserve, and just stand your ground no matter what anyone else says

    1. My first company offered me 10, but i refused and they started explaining how 12 is too much in this market

    2. While on the other hand, i had asked 15 to atlan and then offered 18

    3. So find rich companies i guess

7. Dont leave vanderlande till you find something better

8. To become SDE II

    1. Have regular talks with manager, ask him what SDE II title means for him, this way you actually set expectations

    2. Then try to meet these expectations, so that they pretty much have to promote you

    3. Some things i had to work on - leading projects, mentoring interns, involving in tech planning and stuff for whole team

9. I always had inclination towards big data / data engineering.

10. Send me , ill do roasting (constructive hehe)
