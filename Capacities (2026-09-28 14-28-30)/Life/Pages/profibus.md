# profibus

PROFIBUS (Process Fieldbus) is a digital network that allows communication between field devices and control systems in industrial environments. It's used in manufacturing, process automation, and other applications. 

How it works

- PROFIBUS uses a bus topology, where a central line, or bus, is wired throughout the system. 

- Devices are attached to this central bus. 

- A "token" is passed between masters, and the master holding the token can communicate with slave devices. 

Benefits 

- PROFIBUS is open, vendor-independent, and OSI-compliant.

- It's a rugged fieldbus system for data communication.

- It's used in a variety of applications.

- It can decrease the cost of wiring.

- It can make data communication more robust.
