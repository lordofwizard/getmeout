# kitabicafe


Book cafe, rain's soft hiss,
Pretty girlfriend, sweet kiss.
Watermelon juice, cool bliss,
Cuddles warm, love's abyss.

Sleepy cat, gentle purr,
Rainy day, hearts stir.
Quiet moments, love's allure,
In cafe's embrace, we confer.
