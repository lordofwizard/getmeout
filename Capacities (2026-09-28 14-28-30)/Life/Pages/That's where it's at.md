# That's where it's at

That 9 pm vibe

fancy restaurant on a random weekday

clean, swept and chill.

girl waitresses, well clothed, less makeup, happy face.

no chairs only couch sitting

ac chilled rooms, with towels instead of tissues. 

cozy as fuck, new leather menu cards. 

resonable items, (same price as your fucking MacD or Burger King). 

for decent food. 

No crowd noises, of shit banging, no fucking self service. 

waitress giving you water, chilled but not freezing in a high quality glass.

you get a leather bound menu card no fucking eating stains or overly used. bullshit

you just call for the card by making the rectangle in the air with index fingers

and the card is brought to you by putting it down on the table and then you can pick up. 

NO SOUNDS of banging and chill.

soooth jazz playing in the background.
