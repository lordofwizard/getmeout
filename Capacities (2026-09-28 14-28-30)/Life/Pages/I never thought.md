# I never thought

I never thought these cute convos would heal me so much.

I never thought these pair of caffeinated eyes would mean so much,

I never thought ur touch would soothe me so mch,

I never thought anyone would ever care for me so much.



I never thought Linux and non soydev stuff would be my everyday convo,

I never thought talking to you would be the favorite part of my day,



And I'm grateful to have met u, My Pikachu
