# EQM = Equipment Management

**Equipment management** involves overseeing and maintaining the machinery and tools used in operations, such as forklifts, conveyor systems, and other equipment in a warehouse or production environment. It includes tasks like:

- **Maintenance:** Scheduling and performing regular checks and repairs to ensure equipment functions properly.

- **Inspection:** Regularly inspecting equipment for wear, safety, and performance issues.

- **Inventory:** Tracking the acquisition, usage, and condition of equipment.

- **Training:** Ensuring personnel are trained to operate and handle equipment safely and efficiently.

Effective equipment management helps to minimize downtime, extend the lifespan of equipment, and ensure smooth operations.
