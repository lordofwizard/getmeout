How to dispose of a body:

Step 1. Features

There are three things you need to remember when disposing of a body.

Teeth

Hair

DNA

Teeth can be matched to dental records and then used to identify a victim. Filing them down doesn’t always work, so if you’re planning to leave them in the skull you should get some hefty pliers and twist them out of alignment as well. Take a few out, break the pattern. Alternatively, you could just grind them up to powder and mix them in with your coffee. (Stomach acid will leave practically nothing left of them, and no sense letting the calcium go to waste, right?)

Hair contains DNA, substance history and a lot more information. More than that, it’s practically a map of what happened. You can tell by the root whether it fell out naturally, was pulled out or even whether it came out before or after death. Make sure you don’t leave any stray hairs when you’re dragging the body around, because the last thing you want is the police finding dead guy hair on your kitchen floor.

DNA is difficult to completely dispose of, because it’s in almost every cell in your body. Even a sample of skin from underneath fingernails can be used to form a DNA profile, however these profiles aren’t 100% accurate and one in a million people have identical ones.

Just don’t leave any identifying factors lying around.

Step 2. Destruction

Burying a body is great. It decomposes over time. Make sure that there’s not a body-shaped digging patch in the middle of a lawn though or anything like that. Also, make sure that nobody goes digging there again any time soon.

Acid is good, although you have to be careful of which sort you use. Quite a lot of acids will leave the skeleton and teeth behind (dental records!!!) but as long as they’re strong enough there will be no chance of getting DNA out.

A compound called ‘lye’ (suuuuper cheap, easy to get), when heated, can dissolve a body in a few hours but will leave behind some of the calcium phosphate inside bones. Grind this up and sprinkle it in your garden. Nobody will be any the wiser.

Suphuric and hydrofluoric acid are both useful, but these are really hard to get hold of in the wake of the recent acid attacks (and because they’re used to make bombs sometimes) so this could be a little unbelievable. Stick to what makes sense.

Alternatively, choose a method like grinding the body to paste and burning/eating it in small amounts if you want a really good psychopath villain. Mix the ground-up bones in cement if you feel like it!

Step 3. Disposal

If you used an acid in step 2, you’ll need to find a creative way to dispose of the soup left behind.

Put it in a bin? Nope! This won’t end well.

Pour it in the ground? Nope! You’ll change the pH of the soil and that can provide some (circumstantial) evidence as to where you disposed of it.

Pour it down a drain? Yes! Make sure it’s a manhole cover and you’re not pouring an acid down your metal kitchen pipes…

If you used lye or another method in step 2 you’re pretty safe for disposal.
